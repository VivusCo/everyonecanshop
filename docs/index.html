Who gets stuck in your shop

Three of your customers. Each one gets stuck on something small.

  • She sees poorly. Pale grey text and tiny buttons stop her.

  • She is blind. Her screen reader can only say what has a name.

  • He can't use a mouse. He must always see where he is on the page.

What is the European Accessibility Act?

An EU law that says online shops must be usable for people with disabilities. In short:

  1. 2019

    The EU adopts the law (Directive 2019/882).

  2. 28 June 2025

    It applies to webshops that sell to consumers in the EU.

  3. Now

    Each country checks it with its own authority.

  • Who

    Webshops and shopping apps that sell to consumers in the EU.

  • Not for

    Very small businesses: fewer than 10 employees and at most €2 million yearly turnover (or balance sheet total).

  • What

    Every customer can see or hear the content, use everything with a keyboard or a screen reader, understand it, and buy.

Questions

Does the law apply to my shop?

Most likely, if you sell to consumers in the EU and have 10 or more employees or more than €2 million turnover. This is not legal advice.

Will an accessibility widget solve it?

No. A widget is a toolbar add-on you install on your shop. It sits on top, but the shop underneath stays the same. Buttons and fields without a name still have no name.

How do you test without access?

We use your public shop like a customer: browse, choose, add to cart, go to checkout. We stop before payment.

Is our data safe?

We only see what every visitor sees. No logins, no customer data, no real orders. Our own site uses no cookies.

Do you fix the problems?

Your developers or agency do, with our list of fixes. Afterwards we check again.

What if you find nothing?

Then you get that in writing, free. On real shops this is rare.

Is the report a legal certificate?

No. It is a professional test against the official EU checklist. It shows what to fix first.
